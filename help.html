Help & Guide

Everything you need to master the Aditya Student Photos search tool.

🎯 How to Search

  1. Select your College: Choose between AU (Aditya University), AEC, or ACET using the toggle buttons at the top of the main page.
  2. Enter your search query:
    • Full Roll Number: Type exactly 10 characters (e.g., 23MH1A0501) to load a specific student.
    • Name Search: Type a student's name (e.g., Sagar) to find matches.
    • Prefix Search: Type the starting characters of a roll number to load an entire class batch at once (explained below).
  3. Click Generate: Check the agreement box, hit generate, and the app will do the rest!

🚀 What is a "Prefix Search"?

Think of a prefix as a shortcut. Instead of searching for 60 students one by one, you just type the part of the roll number that the whole class shares.

For AEC / ACET Students:

  • If you are in ACET IT (2023 batch), your full roll number is something like 23MH1A1202.
  • The prefix for your entire class is 23MH1A12.
  • Searching for 23MH1A12 will instantly generate the entire IT batch.

For Aditya University (AU) Students:

  • If you are in AU CSE (2024 batch), your full roll number is something like 24B11CS001.
  • The prefix for your entire class is 24B11CS.
  • Searching for 24B11CS will instantly generate your entire CSE batch.

🧩 Understanding Roll Numbers

Roll numbers are just codes that tell us your joining year, campus, and branch.

1. Traditional Format (AEC & ACET)

Example: 23MH1A1202

23 Year (2023)
MH Campus (ACET)
1A Course (Regular)
12 Branch (IT)
02 Student No.
Lateral Entry Note (AEC/ACET): LE students will have 5A instead of 1A (Example: 24MH5A1201).

2. Aditya University Format (AU)

Example: 24B11CS001

24 Year (2024)
B11 Program (B.Tech)
CS Branch (CSE)
001 Student No.
Lateral Entry Note (AU): LE students will have B21 instead of B11 (Example: 25B21CS001).
